## Description (4000)

Welcome to the shadiest garage in town.

Customers roll in with busted rides. You can fix them honest... or quietly
swap their good parts onto your own build before they notice. Every
"borrowed" part spikes suspicion — push too far and the heat comes down.

RUN THE GARAGE
Inspect each car, fix what's worn, and decide what's worth taking. Watch the
suspicion meter, time it around the owner's glance, and cash out before the
cops roll by.

BUILD A MONSTER
Turn your chop-shop profits into a street weapon — engines, turbos, tires,
nitrous, and a paint booth. Tune it in the build bay, then take it to the strip.

CLIMB THE PINK-SLIP LADDER
Race four rivals in 3D time trials. Win and their car is yours; lose and you
hand over your keys. Work your way up from Granny Shift to the champion.

A NEW HUSTLE EVERY DAY
A daily parts market, contracts to fill, and a crew to hire — there's always
another angle to work.

- Premium: one price, no ads, no in-app purchases

- Plays fully offline, on iPhone and iPad
